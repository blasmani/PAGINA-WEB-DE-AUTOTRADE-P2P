§ 01What it does

Three jobs you stop doing by hand.

Not a bot that trades for you. A desk assistant that handles the repetitive part and leaves every decision to you.

It answers the chat for you

Write your own message templates and choose when each one is sent: when an order opens, when the buyer marks it paid, when a payment proof arrives, when someone asks who the account belongs to. A trigger is marked as handled before the message goes out, so a customer never gets the same notice twice.

Rehearsal mode included

It keeps a real order history

Every order, with the counterparty's verified name, the payment method used and the detail you captured in the chat. Filter by date, asset, currency, status or order-number range, and download it as CSV or as a formatted Excel file with frozen headers, filters and totals.

CSV · XLSX

It defends your rank

The pricing engine watches where your ad sits in the list and moves its price only when it actually falls out of the band you set — never chasing an exact position, which is what looks like price manipulation. It never crosses the price limits you write.

§ 02How it works

Four steps, once.

You do not have to create an account with us, because there is no us in the middle: the app talks straight to the official Binance API with a key you create yourself.

1

Install it

From Microsoft Store, on Windows 10 or 11. It opens in its own window.

2

Paste your API key

Create it in your own Binance account and paste it into Settings. It is encrypted on your PC and never leaves it.

3

Adjust the ready-made templates or write your own, and pick which trigger fires each one. You can try it in rehearsal mode first.

Turn it on

Nothing runs until you switch it on, and it remembers the choice. Close the window and it keeps answering from the tray.

§ 03You stay in control

What it will never do.

This is the part most people ask about first, so it is written down before you install anything.

It never releases crypto

Never

Reviewing a payment proof is a human decision and it stays one: your Binance fund password is not stored anywhere, not even in memory, so no automated process could release funds even if it wanted to.

It does not buy, sell or transfer

Never

It does not deposit or withdraw either. It is not a wallet and not an exchange, and it never takes custody of your funds.

It gives no investment advice

Never

It shows no market prices for speculation and tells you nothing about what to trade.

Only the official Binance API

Always

No scraping and no session cookies — that is what keeps your account safe.

Your data stays on your PC

Always

We run no servers, so nothing the app reads or writes ever reaches us.

§ 05FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to give you my Binance password?

No, and you should never give it to anyone. AutoTrade P2P uses an API key that you create yourself inside your own Binance account, with the permissions you choose. It is encrypted on your PC with Windows DPAPI and tied to your Windows account: copying the files to another computer does not let anyone read them.

What permissions does the API key need?

Two, and no more: Enable Reading, for your orders, your ads and the chat; and Enable Spot & Margin Trading, which is what lets it move the price of your own ads. Leave everything else off, especially Enable Withdrawals: the app does not use it, and leaving it off is what means that even if your key leaked, nobody could take money out of your account. You choose all of this yourself when you create the key.

Can it release crypto for me?

No, and that is deliberate. Checking that a payment really arrived is a judgement call, and the app is built so it cannot make it: your Binance fund password is never stored, so nothing automatic has what it would need.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep using it until the period you already paid for runs out, and Microsoft stops charging you. There is no cancellation fee and nothing to ask us for: it is a switch in your own Microsoft account.

What happens to my history if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. The database is a file on your own PC and it stays there; you can export everything to CSV or Excel before or after the subscription ends.

Does it work while the window is closed?

Yes. Closing the window sends it to the system tray and the engine keeps answering your order chat. To quit completely, right-click the tray icon and choose Exit.

Does it work with the PC turned off?

No. Everything runs on your machine, so with the PC off or asleep nothing gets answered. That is the other side of having no server: nobody but you holds your key.

Is it affiliated with Binance?

No. AutoTrade P2P is an independent product and is not endorsed by, sponsored by or affiliated with Binance. It simply uses Binance's official public API with your own key.

What languages does the app speak?

Eight: English, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified), Hindi, Russian, French and German. It follows your Windows language and you can change it from the side menu.

How do I send a suggestion or report a problem?

There is a Suggestions section in the app's side menu that writes the message for you and opens Telegram, where you can attach screenshots. Or write directly to t.me/AutoTradeP2P.
